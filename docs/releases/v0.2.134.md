# Harness v0.2.134

Desktop release for macOS, Windows and Linux: links work again in a chat pane
that used to be the other side of a split, ⌘W asks once whether closing a
chat should archive it, and a Stop that lands right after you answer an
agent's question now says so. The Harness TUI shares chats with the app, and
⌘⇧P opens the project picker.

## Chats and panes

- Links in a chat pane open again after it becomes the main view, for example
  when you close a split after focusing its second pane, or pick another chat
  in that pane. Before, a left click did nothing and the link menu's **Open in
  Harness** and **Open in external browser** closed without opening anything
  (#456).
- Releasing a text selection over another split pane no longer activates that
  pane (#198).
- ⌘W follows **Archive sessions when closing tabs or panes**: off, it just
  closes; on, it closes and archives. ⌘⌥W always just closes. The first time
  you press ⌘W, Harness asks which you want and remembers it.
- Collapsed tool previews shorten long file paths (relative to the chat's
  folder, `~` for your home folder, `…` for the rest) and keep the command and
  its flags readable. The expanded view and copied text are unchanged (#367).
- ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P off macOS) opens the project picker; it's rebindable in
  Settings → Shortcuts.
- Custom sidebar sections are gone; pins, sorting, compact mode and the
  project and device groups stay (#490).

## Agents

- When a run is stopped right after you answer an agent's question, the chat
  says the task stopped and why, instead of ending on your answer. The
  computer running the chat also logs which device sent the Stop (#149).
- Prompt history (↑) works from a fixed snapshot, so live updates can't move
  or break it, and question, queue and chat-switch editors keep your real
  unsent draft (#516).
- Archiving a chat retires its idle agent so other agents can't wake it, and
  a restore that races an archive resolves in the order you acted (#498).

## TUI

- The Harness TUI joins a running Harness engine as another client: its
  threads are your Harness chats, a chat started there shows in the desktop
  sidebar and on your phone, and turns started elsewhere stream into it live.

## iOS

- The model picker's corner button turns into a checkmark as soon as you pick
  a different model, and VoiceOver reports the selected model (#407).

Mobile builds are distributed separately; this desktop release does not itself
update TestFlight, App Store or Play installations.
